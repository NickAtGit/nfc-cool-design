A row's actions sit behind one trigger at its end. .row-menu is a <details> with a 32px trigger (40px on any touch screen), the fixed trailing control a list's edge comes from. ui.js closes it on an outside click and on Escape, keeps one open at a time, and near the bottom of the window opens it upward rather than off the page. A destructive item goes last, in the danger colour, with a .menu-sep hairline above it, and it asks first. A row's settings (a name, a role) are not menu items: they are a dialog with one Save.
