Block rows and Sortable

Drag from the handle with a mouse or a finger, or hold a finger on a row for 300 ms; until then the list scrolls. On the keyboard, Space lifts, the arrows move, Space drops and Escape cancels, and one live region says each step. Contact details stays first. Delete asks first, then the toast offers Undo for eight seconds.

Repeater

Folded rows

The fields of a folded row do not exist, which is what keeps a 24-dish menu's form light.

Price, link, rating and stepper

A price reads a comma or a point and shows in the page's locale. A link gains its https://, has its host checked and is null when empty. The rating's number is the control, the stars only show it. The stepper is the host's own (the console's seat stepper), kept to its width in a field's column, and its number can be typed.

Segmented control

Hours

One editor, two shapes on the wire: a booking's week of windows, where a day may end at midnight (shown as 00:00, which a native time input can say and a closing time otherwise cannot), and a place's flat rows, where it may not. Closing a day, removing a window and copying to every day all ask first.
A day is the host's switch row, so its switch is drawn like every other switch the host has. A booking's hours are taken by a button, never by opening the form: autosave has no Save moment, and opening a form must not publish a booking.

Days off

Sorted when the focus leaves a row, so a row never jumps from under the date being typed. A row with no date yet is not sent.

Media grid

Actions under the picture, never on it. Three uploads at a time, the size checked first, and a tile moves by mouse, finger or keyboard like any row.
