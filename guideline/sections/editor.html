Block rows and Sortable

Drag from the handle with a mouse or a finger, or hold a finger on a row for 300 ms; until then the list scrolls. On the keyboard, Space lifts, the arrows move, Space drops and Escape cancels, and one live region says each step. Contact details stays first. Delete asks first, then the toast offers Undo for eight seconds.

Repeater

A row with a required value still empty is held back and says so. Removing a row asks first, and past its limit Add says why it stopped.

Folded rows

The fields of a folded row do not exist, which is what keeps a 24-dish menu's form light.

Price, link and rating

A price reads a comma or a point and shows in the page's locale. A link gains its https://, has its host checked and is null when empty. The rating's number is the control, the stars only show it.
