Rows, not a squeezed table. On a phone a table that cannot fit becomes .list-rows: the item's name and its one quiet action on the first line, then one muted line per fact, hairline-divided inside the card with no box in the box. The facts are written for the line, not copied out of the columns.

An empty list keeps its card. The card's head still names the list, so the page has the same cards whether they are empty or not, and the empty state is one sentence: "No domains yet." plus the next step. A filtered list that comes back empty says "No leads match this filter." and offers the way out.

Every grid track is minmax(0, 1fr). .grid-2 is two columns and one on a phone, and a bare 1fr would let one long option in a select widen the page past the screen.
