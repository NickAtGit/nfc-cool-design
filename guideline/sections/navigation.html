The console sidebar. Three modes on the existing four breakpoints, so no new breakpoint enters the system: full with labels at lg and up, a stacked rail with each label under its icon from md to lg, and an off-canvas drawer with a scrim below md. .is-collapsed is how a person collapses the sidebar themselves, and the choice persists. The sidebar follows the theme: the console obeys one palette rather than carrying a second dark surface family that has to be kept in contrast on its own. Below 768px the first example becomes a drawer. Use the burger in its header to open it.
