Labels are hidden visually but stay in the accessibility tree, so a screen reader still reads "Members, 4". The console sidebar. Three modes on the existing four breakpoints, so no new breakpoint enters the system: full with labels at lg and up, a stacked rail with each label under its icon from md to xl, and below md a sticky bar whose burger drops the panel under it, the way the business console has always worked on a phone. Not a drawer: a panel that opens where the thumb already is beats one that slides in from an edge. It sits below md. .is-collapsed is how a person collapses the sidebar themselves, and the choice persists. The sidebar follows the theme: the console obeys one palette rather than carrying a second dark surface family that has to be kept in contrast on its own. Below 768px the first example becomes the bar with its dropdown. Use its own burger to open it.
