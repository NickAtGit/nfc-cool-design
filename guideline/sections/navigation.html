The console sits in .app-shell: the nav card in one column, the page in the other, and the page says its own name in a .page-head, never in a bar. Resize the window to walk the three states; below 768px this nav is the bar and its burger drops the panel.
The console shell. The business console's, as it has always shipped, so a product inherits it rather than rebuilding it. Three states on the existing four breakpoints: the floating column with labels beside icons and the theme switch at its foot at xl and up; a stacked rail with each label under its icon from md to xl; and below md a sticky bar of brand, theme icon and burger, whose burger drops the panel under it as its own card. A dropdown, not a drawer: a panel that opens where the thumb already is beats one that slides in from an edge, and it needs no scrim. One markup for all three: brand, theme icon, burger, panel. The utilities sit INSIDE the panel here (the opposite of the bar), so on a phone they fold with the destinations and on a desktop they sit at the foot of the column. The example is contained in its box, so the column does not stick. Below 768px use its own burger to open the panel.

The person's own rail. .is-collapsed forces the rail at any width and the choice persists in localStorage. Below xl the control is hidden, because the viewport has already made that choice.
