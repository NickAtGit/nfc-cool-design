One alert per tone, from the same ramp as the pills, a native progress bar for anything with a known end, and a skeleton for anything still loading. An alert is for a standing state of the page (a limit reached, a domain that will not verify); it is not how a page says that something just happened.

The toast: one feedback channel

Invite sent to jordan@example.com.
Could not remove the domain. Try again.
Everything that just happened is said in one place. A save, a sent invite, a refused form after a redirect: all of it goes to the one .toast at the foot of the window, never to a list drawn in the page, where on a phone a confirmation landed below the figures and nobody saw it. A success hides itself after five seconds; an error stays until its close button, because it has to be read and may have to be acted on. The page ships the element empty, so the live region exists before anything is announced, and ui.js fills it (showToast) or picks up one the server rendered. A field's own error is not a toast: it is .field-error under the field.
