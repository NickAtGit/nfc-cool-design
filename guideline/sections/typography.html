Two tracking directions. They are the system's signature and they are tokens: negative on display sizes (--tracking-hero, --tracking-display), positive on uppercase micro-labels (--tracking-label, --tracking-eyebrow). Arabic resets tracking to normal, because it is a connected script.

The wordmark

Read and write NFC tags with NFC.cool on your phone.

Basis. The wordmark is a logotype and always reads left to right, including mid-sentence in an Arabic heading. .brand-name sets direction: ltr and unicode-bidi: isolate unconditionally, not under [dir="rtl"], so a consumer that ships no RTL stylesheet still cannot get it wrong. Without the isolation the script .cool tail jumps ahead of NFC and the tail's tuck margins invert. Hit RTL in the bar. The Arabic line reverses; the wordmark inside it does not. A test pins the rule.
Per Brand Manual V1 page 10, the tail is the brand yellow and both elements carry their designated shadows. On a light page the "NFC" half takes the heading colour rather than the manual's white, which is the correct adaptation: white is specified for the logo on the band.
