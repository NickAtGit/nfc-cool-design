The page's cards start here.
One way back, above the title. Every page below a list or a section carries a .crumb, "‹ Parent", first in .page-head-text, named with the parent's own title word for word. There is no back button in the header and no "Back to …" at the foot of the page: two ways back is one too many, and the header's buttons are for the page's own actions. A top-level page and a tabbed section carry none. When the parent is a filtered list, the link keeps its filters.

A thing's pages are tabs. The pages of one card (or one section) sit under its head as the same .section-nav a settings section uses, so they read as siblings and each is one tap from the others. On a phone the strip scrolls: ui.js brings the tab you are on into view on load and fades the edge while more tabs wait past it. Narrow the window to see both.
