Shown, selectable, not editable.

Sixteen pixels, always. Inputs are --text-body because anything smaller makes iOS Safari zoom the page on focus. Focus is the newsletter form's :focus-within ring, generalised to every control, and the field border is its own token so a boundary reads on a card. Read-only is not editable and must not look it: a dashed hairline in the quiet border colour, the alternate fill and secondary ink, so it still reads (and copies) but is plainly not a place to type, in dark too, where the fill alone is nearly the field's own. A setting that is on or off is a switch, painted from the checkbox so the form, the keyboard and the screen reader are untouched; its consequence sits on the line beneath and does not fold.

Stepper

One control at the standard height, with drawn signs. The minus and the plus are inline SVG, not typed: Titillium has no minus sign, so a typed one falls back to another face at another weight and baseline, which is exactly how the console's seat stepper looked off. The figure is --text-md, bold, tabular. The tracks are fixed, so the number field never asks for its min-content width and nothing is clipped off the end.
