1. Colour

Two themes, and the mode flip: interactive elements are brand blue in light mode and brand yellow in dark. Yellow is never text on a light surface.

Surfaces

One real step. --color-bg-alt sits one step below --color-bg, so the alternating section rhythm reads, and muted text still clears 4.5:1 on it.

Text

Three levels of ink. Text, secondary and muted, and all three clear 4.5:1 on every surface they appear on, including the alt surface.

Interactive

Two roles for blue. --color-link is the light stop, for borders, chevrons, icons and hover fills, where 3:1 is the bar. --color-link-text is the dark stop, 5.38:1, for anything that is text, and its hover darkens rather than lightens, so hovering a link never makes it harder to read.

Brand

The band. The gradient is the iOS app's appThemeGradient, dark at the top and light at the bottom, so the web band matches what the product renders. White clears AA at the top stop and holds above the 3:1 large-text floor at the bottom; --on-brand-text-shadow carries body copy over the lower half. Brand Manual V1, page 10, requires the designated shadows on the logo in any case, so the band and the shadow travel together.

The script tail is marked exempt: WCAG 1.4.3 exempts text that is part of a logo or brand name, and this renders the brand name. It is one token so the call can be revisited, and the pattern is never copied onto ordinary text.

Semantic tones

Success Warning Danger Info Neutral
One ramp per tone. Foreground, background and border for success, warning, danger, info and neutral, and every pill, alert and status marker draws from the same five.

2. Typography

One family, Titillium Web, at three weights, with Caveat as the script accent. The ramp below is the whole named ladder.

Two tracking directions. They are the system's signature and they are tokens: negative on display sizes (--tracking-hero, --tracking-display), positive on uppercase micro-labels (--tracking-label, --tracking-eyebrow). Arabic resets tracking to normal, because it is a connected script.

3. Space, shape, elevation, motion

Space scale

A 4px grid that keeps --space-5: 1.25rem (20px), off an 8px grid on purpose: it is the signature card-grid gap, and every grid on the site is spaced by it.

Radius

Six radii. --radius-md (10px) matches the App Store badge corner, which is why buttons use it.

Elevation

Four steps, plus --elev-media, the house treatment for product renders: a cool blue-tinted double shadow by day that becomes a warm accent glow at night. Toggle the theme to see it invert.

Motion

Hover: lift 2
Linked: lift 3
Deliberately tiny. One duration (--transition: .2s ease), one gesture, a hover lift in three tiers, and one entrance: the FAQ answer unfolds when its card opens. No scroll animation and no reveal-on-scroll. Under prefers-reduced-motion the lifts collapse to 0 and the unfold is switched off.

4. Buttons

One component parameterised by six local custom properties, so the same element re-skins by context instead of forking into a variant per surface.

Tiers

Five tiers, one component. The filled primary is the action a surface exists for: Save, Invite, Save Contact. The outlined tier is secondary, ghost is tertiary, and danger is outlined until it is the one destructive action on the surface, when it fills. Store badges stay the marketing hero's CTA; a filled button is for work, not for leaving to the store.

One blue, used as an area. The filled tier fills with #128CF0 and the outlined tier borders with the same value, so they read as one family. White on that fill is 3.48:1, under the 4.5 a 15px label needs; it is a deliberate decision, pinned in the contrast suite with its date so it stays revisitable rather than becoming an invisible regression.

Hover darkens to #0F76CA, which lifts the label to 4.70:1, so the state a person is actively pointing at is the compliant one. The test does not pin a direction, it pins the outcome: hover must never make the label harder to read. In dark mode, where the primary is brand yellow with a near-black label, that same rule makes hover brighten instead.

If it is ever revisited, #0F78CE is the nearest shade of this blue where a white label clears 4.5 at rest.

Sizes and states

One blue on a button. Blue as an area is the light stop; blue as text is the dark stop. On a button the blue is always a fill or a border and never the label, so Save and Cancel border and fill in the same #128CF0. Cancel's label is ink rather than blue: a blue label beside a blue border is two blues on one control, and the light stop is only 3.48:1 as text anyway. A border is a UI boundary, so 3:1 applies and it clears at 3.48. Ghost has no border, so its label carries the affordance and takes the dark stop, like an inline link.

Every state is defined, including :focus-visible: tab through them.

On a brand band

One mechanism. Adding .on-brand re-points the button's local properties and the focus ring. No new classes, no duplicated rules.
